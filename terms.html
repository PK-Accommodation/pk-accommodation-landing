Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 21 September 2026. These terms govern your use of this website and your tenancy with PK Accommodation, operated by P.K MENS PG under ANNAI GENERAL STORE.

1. Eligibility

Our accommodation is for men aged 18 and above. A valid government-issued photo ID is required for identity verification (KYC) before a tenancy is activated.

2. Booking and reservation

  • An enquiry can be converted to a reservation by our staff. No payment is required to reserve a bed.
  • A reservation becomes an active tenancy only once KYC verification, a signed rental agreement, and the applicable payment (joining fee, first month's rent and security deposit) are complete.
  • Your intended room number may be communicated to you in advance, but the specific bed is assigned by our staff.
  • We reserve the right to decline or cancel a reservation before activation, for example if KYC cannot be verified.

3. Rent

  • Rent is billed on a cycle anchored to your own joining date (e.g. joining on the 12th of a month means every subsequent cycle runs 12th to 11th), not a shared calendar date for all tenants.
  • Rent must be paid in full for a cycle — partial payments are not accepted as settling a due.
  • A late fee of 1% of the rent per day applies after a grace period of 12 days from the due date.
  • Rent amounts, and any correction to a wrongly charged or wrongly paid amount, are governed solely by our internal ledger records.

4. Security deposit

  • A security deposit is collected at move-in, amount confirmed at the time of booking.
  • 80% of the deposit may be used to adjust against outstanding dues or damages during your stay; 20% is always retained until final move-out settlement.
  • The deposit (less any deductions) is settled at move-out — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

5. Notice period and lock-in

  • A minimum notice period applies before moving out; the exact number of days is confirmed in your rental agreement.
  • A lock-in penalty (currently ₹500) applies if you leave before the end of your agreed lock-in period, even with valid notice.
  • A separate no-notice penalty (currently ₹1,000) applies if you leave without giving the required notice.
  • These charges are adjusted against your security deposit at move-out settlement.

6. Tenant responsibilities

  • Maintain the room, shared facilities and common areas in good, clean condition.
  • Follow the property's curfew, visitor and guest policy as communicated by on-site staff.
  • No illegal activity, and no possession or use of prohibited substances, on the premises.
  • No sub-letting of your bed/room to another person.
  • Report maintenance issues or damages to staff promptly.
  • Any damage to property caused by a tenant may be charged against the tenant's deposit.

7. Move-out

  • You may give notice to move out at any time; a reason for moving out is optional and never mandatory.
  • Final settlement (dues, deposit refund, any applicable penalties) is completed as part of the move-out process — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

8. Electricity and other charges

At properties where electricity is billed separately, charges are calculated per our stated method and billed periodically. Other charges (e.g. maintenance, short-stay daily rate) are as communicated to you and may be updated by us from time to time; changes do not apply retroactively to already-recorded charges.

9. Cancellations

See our dedicated Cancellation & Refund Policy for how reservation, payment and deposit cancellations are handled.

  • All content on this site belongs to PK Accommodation / P.K MENS PG unless otherwise noted, and may not be reproduced without permission.

11. Disputes and governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of your tenancy or use of this website will be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
