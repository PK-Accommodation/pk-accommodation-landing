Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 21 September 2026. These terms govern your use of this website and your tenancy with PK Accommodation, operated by P.K MENS PG under Annai General Store.

1. Definitions and parties

"We", "us", "our" refers to PK Accommodation, operated by P.K MENS PG under Annai General Store. "You", "your", "tenant" refers to any person booking, reserving, or holding a tenancy with us, or using this website.

2. Eligibility

Our accommodation is for men aged 18 and above. A valid government-issued photo ID is required for identity verification (KYC) before a tenancy is activated.

3. Booking and reservation

  • An enquiry can be converted to a reservation by our staff. No payment is required to reserve a bed.
  • A reservation becomes an active tenancy only once KYC verification, a signed rental agreement, and the applicable payment (joining fee, first month's rent, and security deposit) are complete.
  • Your intended room number may be communicated in advance; the specific bed is assigned by staff.
  • We reserve the right to decline or cancel a reservation before activation — for example, if KYC cannot be verified.

4. KYC and agreement requirement

A tenancy is not active until identity verification (KYC), the signed digital agreement, and the payment declaration described in Section 3 are all complete.

5. Rent

  • Rent is billed on a cycle anchored to your own joining date (e.g. joining on the 12th means every cycle runs 12th to 11th), not a shared calendar date for all tenants.
  • Joining on or before the 14th of a month: no proration for the first cycle.
  • Joining on or after the 15th: your billing may instead be aligned to a calendar-1st cycle, raising one prorated first charge.
  • Rent is due at the start of each cycle.
  • A late fee of 1% of the rent per day applies after a grace period of 12 days from the due date.
  • Rent does not support partial payment — a due amount is either paid in full or remains outstanding. Any advance paid is applied against the next due amount.
  • Rent payments may be refunded or corrected where a genuine billing or entry error is identified, on request with a reason.

6. Security deposit

  • A security deposit is collected at move-in, with the amount confirmed at the time of booking.
  • 80% of the deposit may be used to adjust against outstanding dues or damages during your stay; 20% is always retained until final move-out settlement.
  • The deposit (less any deductions) is settled at move-out — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

7. Notice, move-out charges, and lock-in

  • The required notice period before moving out is 15 days.
  • A no-notice penalty of ₹1,000 applies if you leave without giving the required 15 days' notice.
  • A lock-in penalty of ₹500 applies if you leave before the end of your agreed lock-in period, even with valid notice.
  • A maintenance deduction of ₹500 applies at every move-out, regardless of notice given or the condition of the room.
  • A short-stay charge of ₹500/day applies in two situations: (a) a booking for a stay shorter than our standard minimum term, and (b) remaining in the room past your agreed move-out or notice date, charged per additional day.
  • These charges are adjusted against your security deposit at move-out settlement.
  • Joining fee, short-stay rate, maintenance deduction, and penalty amounts are Owner/Admin-editable and may be revised from time to time. Revisions apply prospectively only and do not affect charges already recorded.

8. Tenant responsibilities

  • Maintain the room, shared facilities, and common areas in good, clean condition.
  • Follow the property's curfew, visitor, and guest policy as communicated by on-site staff.
  • No illegal activity, and no possession or use of prohibited substances, on the premises.
  • No sub-letting of your bed/room to another person.
  • Report maintenance issues or damages to staff promptly.
  • Any damage to property caused by a tenant may be charged against the tenant's deposit.

9. Move-out

  • You may give notice to move out at any time; a reason for moving out is optional and never mandatory.
  • Final settlement (dues, deposit refund, any applicable penalties) is completed as part of the move-out process — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

10. Electricity and other charges

At properties where electricity is billed separately, charges are calculated as a rate applied per occupied bed (never per meter, never per room), and billed periodically. Other charges — maintenance, short-stay rate, joining fee — are as communicated to you and may be updated by us from time to time; changes do not apply retroactively to already-recorded charges.

11. Our right to refuse or end a tenancy

We may decline a reservation or end a tenancy where KYC cannot be verified, where payment remains outstanding beyond the applicable grace period, or in the case of a serious violation of these terms or property rules.
