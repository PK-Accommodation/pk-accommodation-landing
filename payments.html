Payments & Booking

What you pay for, and how

This page explains what a booking with us involves, what payments are collected, and how the process flows from enquiry to move-in.

Booking flow

From enquiry to move-in

STEP 1

Enquiry

You tell us your preferred property, room type and move-in date.

STEP 2

Reservation

We reserve a bed for you. No payment is required to reserve.

STEP 3

KYC & agreement

ID verification and a signed rental agreement, before any payment is taken.

STEP 4

Payment

Joining fee, first month's rent and security deposit are collected.

STEP 5

Move-in

Once payment is confirmed by staff, your stay is activated and you move in.

How to pay

Accepted payment modes

Payments are currently collected via UPI or bank transfer, confirmed and receipted by our staff. Online card/UPI checkout on this website is being added - this page will be updated once it's live.

UPI

Pay via any UPI app to the ID shared by staff.

Bank transfer

Direct transfer to our bank account, details shared on request.

Cash

Accepted in person at the property, with a receipt issued.

All payments are recorded against your account and a receipt is issued for every payment. No partial payments are accepted against a due - a due is either paid in full or remains outstanding.
