Payments & Booking

What you pay for, and how

This page explains what a booking with us involves, what payments are collected, and how the process flows from enquiry to move-in.

Booking flow

From enquiry to move-in

STEP 1

Enquiry

You tell us your preferred property, room type and move-in date.

STEP 2

Reservation

We reserve a bed for you. No payment is required to reserve.

STEP 3

KYC & agreement

ID verification and a signed rental agreement, before any payment is taken.

STEP 4

Payment

Joining fee, first month's rent and security deposit are collected.

STEP 5

Move-in

Once payment is confirmed by staff, your stay is activated and you move in.

What you can pay for

Charges you may see

The exact amount for each charge depends on the property, room and sharing type, and is confirmed to you before payment. Rates below reflect our standard policy and are admin-editable, so may change from time to time.

Joining fee
A one-time fee (currently ₹150) charged when a tenancy is activated.
Monthly rent
Billed on a cycle anchored to your own joining date (e.g. join on the 12th, billed the 12th of every month), not a shared calendar date.
Security deposit
Collected at move-in. 80% of the deposit is usable toward dues or damages during your stay; 20% is always retained and refunded only at final settlement.
Electricity
Billed at properties where electricity is charged separately, based on usage.
Short-stay / daily rate
For brief stays or extending a few extra days beyond a cycle (currently ₹500/day).
Late fee
1% of rent per day, applied only after a 12-day grace period from the due date.
Lock-in / no-notice charges
Applicable only if you leave before your lock-in period ends, or leave without the required notice — see Terms & Conditions.
How to pay

Accepted payment modes

Payments are currently collected via UPI or bank transfer, confirmed and receipted by our staff. Online card/UPI checkout on this website is being added — this page will be updated once it's live.

UPI

Pay via any UPI app to the ID shared by staff.

Bank transfer

Direct transfer to our bank account, details shared on request.

Cash

Accepted in person at the property, with a receipt issued.

All payments are recorded against your account and a receipt is issued for every payment. No partial payments are accepted against a due — a due is either paid in full or remains outstanding.
