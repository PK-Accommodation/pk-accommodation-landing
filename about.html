About Us

Who we are

PK Accommodation is the customer-facing brand of P.K MENS PG — a Chennai hostel and PG accommodation business that has been housing men across the city, offering clean, secure and affordably priced stays for students and working professionals.

What we provide

P.K MENS PG operates men-only PG and hostel accommodation with 2 to 5-sharing rooms, across six locations in Chennai. We handle the day-to-day of hostel living — rent collection, maintenance, housekeeping, and tenant support — so residents can focus on their studies or work.

Who we serve

Our residents are primarily students and working professionals relocating to Chennai for college or a job, who need a dependable, move-in-ready room without the overhead of setting up an independent household.

Our Locations

Present across Chennai

P.K MENS PG runs properties in West Mambalam, Ambattur, Saidapet, Mogappair West, Choolaimedu and Arumbakkam — each independently staffed and managed. See the Properties page for full addresses and details.
