Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026. This policy explains what happens to each type of payment if a reservation is cancelled, or a tenancy ends.

1. Reservation (before payment)

A reservation can be cancelled at any time before your tenancy is activated, at no cost — no payment is ever collected at the reservation stage, so there is nothing to refund.

2. Joining fee

The joining fee is collected only once a tenancy is activated (i.e. once you have moved in or are about to). It is non-refundable once collected, as it is a one-time administrative charge for onboarding.

3. Rent

  • Rent paid for a billing cycle is not refunded for early, voluntary move-out part-way through that cycle.
  • Rent charged or collected in error is corrected or refunded by our staff on request — genuine entry mistakes are always fixed.
  • Advance rent paid ahead of a cycle is automatically applied to the next due cycle; any unused advance at move-out is refunded as part of final settlement.

4. Security deposit

  • 80% of your security deposit is usable during your stay to adjust against outstanding dues or damages.
  • The remaining 20% is always retained and is never adjusted against routine dues.
  • At move-out, the usable portion of the deposit (less any deductions for pending dues, damages, or applicable penalties) is refunded to you.
  • Refunds are processed after move-out settlement is completed, typically within 7–15 working days, to the same payment mode used originally (bank transfer/UPI) unless otherwise agreed.

5. Lock-in and no-notice charges

If applicable (see Terms & Conditions), the lock-in penalty and/or no-notice penalty are deducted from your security deposit at move-out settlement — these are not separately refundable.

6. Electricity, mess and other charges

Any outstanding electricity, mess, maintenance or other charges are settled against your ledger and, where applicable, adjusted against your deposit at move-out. These charges are not separately refundable once billed for services already used.

7. Cancellation by us

If we are unable to honour a confirmed booking (for example, a property or room becomes unavailable), any payment already collected for that booking will be refunded in full.

8. How to request a refund or correction

Write to tech@pkaccommodation.in or contact your property's staff directly with your name, property, and the payment/ledger detail in question. We aim to resolve refund and correction requests promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the version in effect at the time of your payment or move-out will apply.
