FAQs

Common questions

Still unsure about something? Contact us and we'll answer directly.

Do I need to pay anything to reserve a bed?
No. A reservation needs no payment at all - we hold the bed for you as soon as staff confirm it. Payment (joining fee, first month's rent and deposit) is only collected once your tenancy is activated, after KYC and a signed agreement.
What do I need to complete before moving in?
A valid government-issued photo ID for KYC verification, a signed rental agreement, and payment of the joining fee, first month's rent and security deposit. Once staff confirm payment, your tenancy is activated and you can move in.
What payment modes do you accept?
UPI, bank transfer and cash, all recorded against your account with a receipt issued. Online card/UPI checkout on this website is being added.
How is my rent billed?
Rent is billed on a cycle anchored to your own joining date rather than a shared calendar date - for example, joining on the 12th means every cycle runs the 12th to the 11th of the next month.
What happens if I pay rent late?
A late fee of 1% of the rent per day applies, but only after a 12-day grace period from the due date.
How much notice do I need to give before moving out?
15 days' notice. A reason for moving out is optional, never mandatory. See our Terms & Conditions for notice period, lock-in and move-out charges.
Is my security deposit refundable?
80% of your deposit is usable during your stay to adjust against dues or damages; 20% is always retained. The usable portion, less any deductions, is refunded after move-out settlement - see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
Do you offer single rooms, or only shared rooms?
Both. Room options range from single occupancy to 8-sharing, and vary by property - enquire for what's available at your preferred location.
