Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026. This policy applies to pkaccommodation.in and to tenant management activities carried out by PK Accommodation, operated by P.K MENS PG under Annai General Store (Udyam Reg. No. UDYAM-TN-02-0260847), and is written specifically for this business — it is not a generic template.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how PK Accommodation, operated by P.K MENS PG under Annai General Store ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information belonging to website visitors, enquirers, and tenants and prospective tenants of our PG/hostel properties in Chennai.

2. Information we collect

Identity and contact

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Photograph
  • Email address
  • Current and permanent address

Identity/KYC data

  • Last 4 digits of your Aadhaar number — this is the only part of the Aadhaar number we retain
  • Name, date of birth, gender, and address as printed on the Aadhaar document, collected as part of identity verification
  • Where identity verification is completed via DigiLocker (once enabled), the same data fields fetched with your consent

Tenancy information

  • Property, room, and bed assigned; joining date; rent amount; agreement details; guardian/emergency contact details where provided

Payment information

  • Declared payment details: amount, date, mode (UPI/bank transfer/card), and a reference number
  • Once online payment collection is enabled: transaction reference data returned by our payment gateway (transaction ID, order ID, bank/UPI reference). We do not receive or store your UPI ID/VPA, card number, UPI PIN, or bank login — these are entered directly on the payment gateway's own page.

Communications

  • Messages exchanged with us over WhatsApp, phone, email, or the enquiry form on this website

Website usage data

  • Basic technical information (such as browser type) that may be logged by our hosting provider for security purposes

3. Sensitive data — Aadhaar

Aadhaar information is handled as a distinct, higher-sensitivity category, separate from ordinary contact data. Only the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar number are retained in our records. The full number is used solely at the point of identity verification and is not stored beyond that. Access to this information is restricted to authorised staff.

4. Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and process bookings/reservations
  • To verify tenant identity as required for hostel/PG occupancy
  • To create and manage a tenancy — rent billing, deposit tracking, agreements, and ledger records
  • To communicate with tenants about dues, notices, receipts, and property matters, including over WhatsApp
  • To process payments once online payment collection is enabled
  • To meet accounting, tax, and other legal/regulatory record-keeping requirements

5. Consent

Submitting the enquiry form, completing KYC, or signing the tenancy agreement constitutes consent to the collection and use of your personal data for the purposes stated in this policy. Consent for Aadhaar/KYC data is taken separately, at the point of KYC.

You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us. Withdrawal may mean we can no longer provide accommodation to you, since a valid tenancy requires KYC and payment records to remain in place.

6. How we collect information

  • Directly from you — the enquiry form, WhatsApp, phone, email, or in person at onboarding
  • Via documents you upload for identity verification
  • Once enabled, via DigiLocker, with your consent
  • Once enabled, via transaction data returned by our payment gateway

7. How we store and protect information

Tenant and payment records are stored on Supabase (secured, access-controlled database and authentication infrastructure). Access is scoped by staff role — Manager and Staff accounts can only view records for the specific properties they are assigned to; Owner and Admin have unrestricted access. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

8. Data retention

We retain tenancy and ledger records, including KYC information, for as long as needed for tenancy administration and for accounting/legal record-keeping purposes, even after a tenancy ends.

9. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We share limited information only with:

  • Payment gateway (once online payment collection is enabled) — solely to process a payment you initiate. No access to card numbers, UPI PIN, or bank login credentials.
  • e-KYC/DigiLocker provider (once enabled) — solely to verify identity documents you provide.
  • WhatsApp Business Platform / Meta — used to deliver rent reminders, receipts, and property announcements. Message and call content is not shared with Meta, but your phone number and messaging metadata are processed by Meta to deliver the message and may be used by Meta for its own products.
  • Our hosting/database provider (Supabase) — processes data on our behalf; does not use it for its own purposes.
  • Government or regulatory authorities — where required by law.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, update, or request erasure of your personal information, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations (for example, we cannot delete financial ledger records required to be retained for accounting purposes). You also have the right to nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity, and the right to raise a grievance as set out below.
