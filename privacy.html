Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026. This policy applies to pkaccommodation.in and to PK Accommodation's / P.K MENS PG's tenant management activities, and is written specifically for our business — it is not a generic template.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how PK Accommodation, operated by P.K MENS PG under ANNAI GENERAL STORE ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, stores and protects personal information belonging to website visitors, enquirers, tenants and prospective tenants of our PG/hostel properties in Chennai.

2. Information we collect

Depending on your relationship with us — website visitor, enquirer, or tenant — we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email address, current and permanent address.
  • Identity/KYC information: government ID details including Aadhaar number, and a copy or image of the ID document, collected to verify identity as part of tenant onboarding.
  • Tenancy information: property, room and bed assigned, joining date, rent amount, agreement details, guardian/emergency contact details where provided.
  • Payment information: rent, deposit and other payment records, including amount, date, mode (UPI/bank transfer/cash) and reference number. We do not store your card, UPI PIN, or bank login credentials — payments are made directly through your own banking/UPI app.
  • Communications: messages exchanged with us over WhatsApp, phone, email or the enquiry form on this website.
  • Verification data: where enabled, identity information obtained via e-KYC/DigiLocker-based verification services, used solely to confirm the identity documents you provide.
  • Website usage data: basic technical information (such as browser type) that may be logged by our hosting provider for security purposes.

3. Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and process bookings/reservations.
  • To verify tenant identity as required for hostel/PG occupancy and local regulatory expectations.
  • To create and manage a tenancy — rent billing, deposit tracking, agreements, and ledger records.
  • To communicate with tenants about dues, notices, receipts and property matters, including over WhatsApp.
  • To maintain records required for our own accounting and, where applicable, for verification with payment and identity-verification partners.

4. Aadhaar and other government ID

Where an Aadhaar number or other government ID is collected for KYC purposes, it is stored securely and used only to verify your identity and to maintain accurate tenant records. We do not publish, sell, or use this information for any purpose unrelated to tenancy verification and legal compliance. Access to full ID numbers is restricted to authorised staff.

5. How we store and protect information

Tenant and payment records are stored in a secured, access-controlled database. Only staff with a legitimate operational need — and scoped to the properties they manage — can view tenant records. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Identity-verification providers (e.g. an e-KYC/DigiLocker service), solely to verify documents you provide.
  • Payment processors or gateways, once online payment collection is enabled, solely to process a payment you initiate.
  • Messaging providers (e.g. WhatsApp Business/Meta), solely to deliver rent reminders, receipts and property communications to you.
  • Government or regulatory authorities, where required by law.

7. WhatsApp communication

If you communicate with us over WhatsApp, your phone number and message content are used to respond to you and, where relevant, to send rent reminders, receipts and onboarding links. You can ask us to stop WhatsApp communication at any time by contacting us.

8. Data retention

We retain tenant records, including ledger and KYC information, for as long as needed for tenancy administration, accounting, and any applicable legal retention requirement, even after a tenancy ends.

9. Your rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal and record-keeping obligations (for example, we cannot delete financial ledger records that must be retained for accounting purposes). Write to tech@pkaccommodation.in for any such request.

10. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Any cookies set are limited to what's strictly necessary for the site to function.

11. Children's information

Our accommodation and services are intended for adults (18 years and above). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

13. Contact us

For any privacy-related question or request, contact us at tech@pkaccommodation.in or +91 98765 43210.
